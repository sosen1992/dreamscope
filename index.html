Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

"សុបិន្ត Dream Fortune" respects and values your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and Use

  • Dream Description & Zodiac Sign: Used solely to process and deliver real-time astrological dream interpretations and traditional numerology predictions via our AI engine. We do not persist or monetize your personal dream logs.
  • Voice Input (Speech-to-Text): If you use the microphone feature, audio data is transcribed in real-time using native platform engines (Google Speech Services or Apple Speech Framework). We do not record or store your voice data.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our app incorporates third-party SDKs to provide core functionalities and monetization:

  • Unity Ads / Meta Audience Network: Used to display banner and interstitial ads. These networks may collect anonymous identifiers (such as Advertising IDs) to deliver personalized ads in compliance with their privacy guidelines.
  • DeepInfra API: Securely processes AI prompts for dream analysis without storing user identities.

3. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your interactions. All communications between the app and external APIs are strictly encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/SSL).

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is not targeted at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from minors.
